Biography

Soprano Henrike Legner has been hailed by the Süddeutsche Zeitung as “revelation for the Baroque repertoire.” This reflects her particular interest in the concert repertoire, which spans major oratorios, cantatas and masses (by Bach, Handel, Haydn and Mozart) as well as the classical art song.

The young singer enjoys a close collaboration with pianist Gerold Huber. Together they have given numerous song recitals, including at the Richard Strauss Institute in Garmisch-Partenkirchen and in the Allerheiligen-Hofkirche (All Saints' Court Church) in Munich. In October 2025 they released a CD of songs by Felix Mendelssohn, some of them little known, on GENUIN classics, which was nominated for the German Record Critics' Award (Preis der Deutschen Schallplattenkritik). She has also given recitals with pianist James Cheung, including in London and at Elmau.

As a soloist, she has performed with ensembles including members of the WDR Funkhausorchester and the Munich Radio Orchestra.

In 2026 she sang La Musica in Monteverdi's L'Orfeo at the Landshut Hofmusiktage early music festival under Kristin von der Goltz, with Christian Gerhaher in the title role. That year she also sang the Hare in Richard Wilds' children's opera Mondbär (Moon Bear) with the Bavarian State Opera, and took part in the Baroque Vocal Academy in Mainz with Alessandro De Marchi.

At the 50th German Music Competition (Deutscher Musikwettbewerb) in 2026, Henrike won a scholarship and was selected for its concert promotion programme for the 2027/28 season. Together with pianist Utako Endo, she won 1st Prize at the "music matters" competition.

Henrike completed her bachelor's degree in London and Padua, and her master's degree at the Bavarian Theatre Academy August Everding and the University of Music and Performing Arts Munich (HMTM) with Christiane Iven. While there, she appeared in Handel's Ariodante, ter Schiphorst's Die Gänsemagd (The Goose Girl) and Cavalli's Il Giasone. In 2025 she sang Gretel in Humperdinck's Hänsel und Gretel at the HMTM. She also studies with Brigitte Fassbaender.

Alongside classical singing, Henrike is interested in interdisciplinary performance formats. Her concept "MetrOstinato" won an award at XPLORE 2024, a competition for new concert formats.
